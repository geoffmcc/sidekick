# Assumptions and Unknowns

This pack projects assumptions and unknowns from Sidekick's existing context, typed memory, and handoff stores. It does not introduce a fourth persistence layer. Caller-supplied lists remain in the response only; durable facts belong in the canonical stores through their governed tools.

Unknowns are evidence gaps, not predictions or facts. Resolve them with current repository, research evidence, health, snapshot, or operational checks before using them in a decision.
